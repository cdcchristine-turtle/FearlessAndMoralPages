Anonymity

For our purposes, anonymity here is that a story's author or meeting-maker's meme, be non-identifiable, unreachable, or untrackable. This anonymity may allow contributors to feel safe revealing feelings or experiences without fear of shame or embarrassment.

Because online material can provide a sense of belonging for those physically isolated, the stories, memes, and other material disclosed anonymously are made available. Anonymity also prevents negative or taboo discussion from tarnishing the reputation of the author.

If you are interested in sharing your story anonymously, please contact us for more information. If you seek help, please reach out.
